# Kasturi Baag
### Chandwad, Nashik

---

*Some things grow not from ambition, but from love.*

---

What you're holding was grown on a small farm in Chandwad, Nashik — 3+ acres, 2,000+ trees, and no chemicals. Ever.

The farm has mangoes, coconut, chickoo, papaya, custard apple, mahogani, avocado, litchi, and banana. But at this time of year, it's all about the Hapus.

My father Dagaji spent 35 years as a schoolteacher. When he retired, he didn't rest — he started farming. My mother Suman, also a retired teacher, tends to it with the same quiet dedication she gave to her classrooms. My sister Prerana is at VIT Vellore, studying engineering. And I'm Jayesh — CoEP and IIM Lucknow — the one who handles the selling, the logistics, and occasionally the eating.

We don't spray chemicals. We don't rush ripening. We wait.

Kasturi Baag was never a business plan. It was a calling.

We hope you taste the difference.
